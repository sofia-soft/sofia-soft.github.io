Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about website and web application development.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the scope and functionality of the project — a corporate website, a web application, or a SaaS platform. After a free consultation, you receive a clear and specific quote with no hidden fees.

How long does it take to build a website?

A corporate website usually takes between 2 and 6 weeks. Larger web applications and SaaS projects are planned in stages, and you get a clear timeline at the very start of the project.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work fully remotely with clients in the UK and across Europe. All communication is in English — via email, phone, or video calls — and our Bulgarian base means competitive rates without compromising on quality.

Is SEO included?

Yes. Every website we build is technically optimized for search engines — fast loading, mobile compatibility, structured data, and proper semantic structure.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We don't disappear after launch — we offer ongoing support, content updates, and development of new features as your business grows.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We take on redesigns and modernization of existing websites — improving speed, design, and SEO, as well as adding new functionality.
