Frequently asked questions

Questions about custom software development

Answers to common questions before a project begins.

What is custom software?

It is software developed specifically for your business, rather than an off-the-shelf product with fixed limitations. It follows your processes and can grow with you.

How much does custom software development cost?

The cost depends on the project's scope and process complexity. After an analysis and free consultation, you receive a clear proposal and timeline with no hidden fees.

Can the software integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We build secure API integrations with accounting software, inventory systems, CRM platforms and external services, keeping your data connected.

Do you support the software after implementation?

Yes. We provide long-term support, updates and new functionality as your business develops.
