Frequently asked questions

Questions about website development

Answers to common questions before a project begins.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the type and scope — a business website, landing page or more complex solution. After a free consultation, you receive a clear proposal with no hidden fees.

How long does website development take?

A business website usually takes between two and six weeks, depending on the content and functionality. You receive a clear timeline at the beginning.

Do you work with clients outside Sofia?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across Bulgaria and abroad through email, phone and video calls.

Will the website be optimised for Google?

Yes. Every website includes technical search optimisation, fast loading, mobile compatibility, structured data and correct semantic markup.
