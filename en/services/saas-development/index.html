Building SaaS platforms from the ground up

SaaS (Software as a Service) is software customers use online through a subscription, without installing it. We build these platforms around your idea — from the architecture and multi-tenant model to payment and subscription integration, administration and analytics.

We focus on scalability, security and stability so your product can support a growing number of users. We work with clients in Bulgaria and abroad on both new products and existing platforms.

  • Multi-tenant architecture
  • Subscriptions and online payments
  • User accounts, roles and permissions
  • Administration and analytics
  • APIs and external integrations
  • Cloud hosting, security and monitoring

Frequently asked questions

Questions about SaaS development

Answers to common questions before a project begins.

What exactly is a SaaS platform?

SaaS is software users access online through a subscription, without installing it. We design and build these platforms around your product idea.

How much does SaaS product development cost?

It depends on the scope and functionality. We usually begin with a clearly defined MVP, optimise the investment and develop the product in stages. You receive a specific proposal after a free consultation.

Can the platform support many users?

Yes. We design for scalability from the beginning so the product remains stable as users and data grow.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. SaaS products require continuous development, so we provide long-term support, monitoring and new functionality as your business evolves.
